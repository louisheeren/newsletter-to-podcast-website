Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Newsletter to Podcast is a personal application that converts email newsletters into audio briefings.

Data access and use

With account authorisation, the application uses read-only Gmail access to retrieve newsletter emails. Newsletter content is processed using AI services to generate summaries, podcast scripts and audio.

Storage and sharing

Generated podcast episodes and processing metadata are stored using cloud services. Newsletter content may be transmitted to third-party AI providers for processing. Published podcast episodes may be publicly accessible.

Data retention

Processing records and generated episodes may be retained until manually deleted. Access can be revoked through Google Account permissions.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator using the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
